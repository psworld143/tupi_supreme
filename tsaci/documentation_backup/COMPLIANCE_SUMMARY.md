## 🎯 Critical Gaps (Must Fix)

### 1. ❌ Municipal Water Treatment Focus - NOT EMPHASIZED
- **Required:** "Premium Activated Carbon Solutions **for Municipal Water Treatment**"
- **Current:** Generic "Premium Activated Carbon Solutions"
- **Impact:** Missing PRIMARY target market emphasis

### 2. ❌ 2mm Granulated Specification - NOT MENTIONED
- **Required:** Display "2mm below (granulated)" prominently
- **Current:** Specification not mentioned anywhere on website
- **Impact:** Key product specification completely missing

### 3. ❌ Products Page Structure - WRONG
- **Required:** Product-based (Granulated Activated Carbon PRIMARY, Coconut Husk Products)
- **Current:** Application-based (Water Treatment, Air Purification, Industrial)
- **Impact:** Doesn't match requirements structure

### 4. ❌ Case Studies Page - MISSING
- **Required:** Municipal water treatment success stories
- **Current:** Page doesn't exist
- **Impact:** Missing credibility building for municipal clients

### 5. ❌ Coconut Husk Products - NOT SHOWN
- **Required:** Coconut Husk Chips, Coconut Pit sections
- **Current:** No mention of husk products
- **Impact:** Missing product line entirely

### 6. ❌ Document Downloads - NOT IMPLEMENTED
- **Required:** Technical data sheets (PDF downloads)
- **Current:** No download functionality
- **Impact:** Missing essential B2B feature

### 7. ❌ Municipal Inquiry Option - MISSING FROM CONTACT FORM
- **Required:** "Municipal Water Treatment Inquiry" in dropdown
- **Current:** Generic inquiry types
- **Impact:** Can't track municipal leads specifically
