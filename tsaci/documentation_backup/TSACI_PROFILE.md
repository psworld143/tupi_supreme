## Products & Services

### 1. Activated Carbon Products

#### **Granulated Activated Carbon**
- **Specifications:** 
  - Particle size: 2mm below (granulated)
  - Type: Granular activated carbon (GAC)
- **Production Process:**
  - Derived from coconut husk processing
  - Waste charcoal (dust) used as fuel for boiler
  - Steam generated from waste charcoal powers production processes
- **Applications:**
  - Municipal water treatment facilities
  - Industrial wastewater treatment
  - Air purification systems
  - Pharmaceutical processing
  - Food & beverage industry

### 2. Coconut Husk Products

#### **Coconut Husk Chips**
- **Type:** Growing medium
- **Applications:**
  - Horticulture
  - Agriculture
  - Gardening
- **Benefits:**
  - Sustainable alternative to peat moss
  - Excellent water retention
  - Natural fiber composition

#### **Coconut Pit**
- **Type:** Growing medium for horticulture
- **Applications:**
  - Professional horticulture
  - Greenhouse operations
  - Agricultural cultivation
- **Specialty:** Premium grade for specialized growing applications
