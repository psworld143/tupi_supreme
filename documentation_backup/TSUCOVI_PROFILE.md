## Products & Services

### 1. Coconut Water Concentrate (Frozen)

#### **Product Specifications:**
- **Type:** Frozen concentrate
- **Freezing Point:** 16 degrees
- **Core Temperature:** -18 degrees (storage)
- **Production Ratio:** 15 liters raw coconut water → 1 liter concentrate
- **Concentration Level:** 15:1 ratio

#### **Production Process:**
- **Method:** Tube evaporator system
- **Technology:** Steam-based evaporation
- **Equipment:** Tube with steam outside to evaporate water
- **Result:** 1 liter concentrated coconut water from 15 liters raw

#### **Applications:**
- Food & beverage industry
- Juice manufacturers
- Sports drinks
- Health products
- Retail food products

#### **Storage & Handling:**
- Frozen storage required
- Core temperature: -18 degrees
- Maintains quality and nutritional properties

---

### 2. Coconut Cream (Frozen)

#### **Product Specifications:**
- **Type:** Frozen coconut cream
- **Fat Content:** 30%
- **Freezing Point:** 16 degrees
- **Core Temperature:** -18 degrees (storage)
- **Quality:** Premium grade

#### **Production Process:**
1. Extraction from fresh coconut meat
2. Processing to achieve 30% fat content
3. Pressing to extract cream
4. **Waste Utilization:** Pressed pulp conveyed to oil mill for additional oil extraction
5. Freezing to maintain quality

#### **By-Product Processing:**
- Pressed pulp → Oil mill → Additional oil extraction
- Maximizes value from raw material
- Zero-waste approach

#### **Applications:**
- Food manufacturing
- Confectionery industry
- Beverage production
- Culinary applications
- Retail products

---

### 3. Crude Coconut Oil

#### **Product Specifications:**
- **Type:** Unrefined coconut oil
- **Raw Material:** Brown coconut husk/pant
- **Processing:** Cold-pressed extraction
- **Quality:** Natural, unrefined

#### **Production Process:**
1. **Source:** Brown coconut husk/pant (outer shell)
2. **Method:** Conveyed to oil mill
3. **Extraction:** Pressed to extract crude oil
4. **Quality:** Natural, unrefined coconut oil

#### **Applications:**
- Further processing into RBD oil
- Natural oil products
- Industrial applications
- Food manufacturing raw material

---

### 4. RBD Coconut Oil

#### **Product Specifications:**
- **Type:** Refined, Bleached, Deodorized coconut oil
- **RBD Process:** Refined, Bleached, Deodorized
- **Quality:** Premium, food-grade
- **Raw Material:** Dried copra

#### **Production Process:**
1. **Preheating:** Oil must be preheated using conditioner cooker
   - Uses heat for proper conditioning
   - Prepares oil for refinement process
2. **Raw Material:** Dried copra serves as primary material for oil mill
3. **Refinement Process:**
   - Refining to remove impurities
   - Bleaching to improve color
   - Deodorizing to remove odors
4. **Result:** Premium RBD coconut oil

#### **Production Equipment:**
- Conditioner cooker (with heat system)
- Oil mill for extraction
- Refinement equipment
- Quality control systems

#### **Applications:**
- Food manufacturing
- Culinary uses
- Cosmetics industry
- Industrial applications
- Retail products

## Operations & Production

### Integrated Processing Flow:

1. **Coconut Water Processing:**
   - Raw coconut water extraction
   - Tube evaporator concentration (15:1)
   - Freezing (-18° core temp)
   - Result: Frozen coconut water concentrate

2. **Coconut Cream Processing:**
   - Cream extraction from coconut meat
   - Processing to 30% fat content
   - Pressing operation
   - Pressed pulp → Oil mill (additional oil extraction)
   - Freezing (-18° core temp)

3. **Oil Processing:**
   - **Crude Oil:** From brown husk/pant via oil mill
   - **RBD Oil:** From dried copra
     - Preheating via conditioner cooker
     - Refinement, bleaching, deodorizing
   - Quality control and packaging

### Equipment & Technology:

- **Tube Evaporators:** Steam-based water concentration
- **Conditioner Cookers:** Oil preheating with heat system
- **Oil Mills:** Extraction and processing
- **Freezing Systems:** -18° core temperature storage
- **Quality Control:** Specifications monitoring

## Processing Flow Details

### Complete Coconut Utilization:

1. **Coconut Water** → Tube evaporator → Concentrated (15:1) → Frozen (-18°)
2. **Coconut Meat** → Cream extraction (30% fat) → Pressed pulp → Oil mill → Additional oil
3. **Brown Husk/Pant** → Oil mill → Crude coconut oil
4. **Dried Copra** → Conditioner cooker (preheat) → Oil mill → RBD coconut oil
5. **Coconut Husk** → Transferred to TSACI for activated carbon production

### Waste Utilization:
- Pressed pulp from cream → Additional oil extraction
- Complete utilization of all coconut parts
- Zero-waste operation model
