## Recommended Structure

### Recommended: Separate Websites

**Benefits:**
1. **Clear Brand Identity:** Each company has distinct brand
2. **Focused SEO:** Better keyword targeting per site
3. **Streamlined UX:** Users see only relevant content
4. **Better Analytics:** Separate tracking per business
5. **Future Flexibility:** Easier to expand independently

**Structure:**
- `tsaci.com` or `tupisupreme-carbon.com` - TSACI website
- `tsucovi.com` or `tupisupreme-coconut.com` - TSUCOVI website

**Alternative:** Same domain, separate paths
- `tupisupreme.com/activated-carbon/` - TSACI
- `tupisupreme.com/coconut-products/` - TSUCOVI
