# Business Context & Goals
## Tupi Supreme Coco Ventures Incorporated & Tupi Supreme Activated Carbon, Inc.

---

## Company Structure

### TSUCOVI - Tupi Supreme Coco Ventures Incorporated
**Primary Focus:** Coconut-based products and derivatives

### TSACI - Tupi Supreme Activated Carbon, Inc.
**Primary Focus:** Activated carbon and coconut husk products

**Relationship:** Integrated operations that utilize all parts of the coconut

---

## Core Business Philosophy

**"We Use All Parts of the Coconut"**

The company operates on a zero-waste principle, maximizing the value from every part of the coconut through integrated operations.

---

## Products & Operations

### TSUCOVI Products

#### 1. **Coconut Water Concentrate (Frozen)**
- **Specifications:** 
  - Freezing point: 16 degrees
  - Core temperature: -18 degrees (same as water concentrate)
  - Production: 15 liters raw water → 1 liter concentrate
  - Process: Tube evaporator with steam outside to evaporate water

#### 2. **Coconut Cream (Frozen)**
- **Specifications:**
  - Fat content: 30%
  - Freezing point: 16 degrees
  - Core temperature: -18 degrees
- **Process:**
  - Pressed pulp from cream is conveyed to oil mill to extract additional oil

#### 3. **Crude Coconut Oil**
- **Raw Material:** Brown coconut husk/pant
- **Process:** Conveyed to oil mill, pressed to extract oil

#### 4. **RBD Coconut Oil**
- **RBD:** Refined, Bleached, Deodorized
- **Process:** 
  - Oil must be preheated using conditioner cooker that uses heat
  - Dried copra serves as raw material for oil mill

### TSACI Products

#### 1. **Activated Carbon**
- **Type:** Granulated
- **Specifications:** 2mm below (granulated)
- **Waste Utilization:** Waste charcoal (dust) used as fuel for boiler
- **Process:** Waste charcoal used to generate steam for cooking products

#### 2. **Coconut Husk Products**
- **Coconut Husk Chips** - Growing medium
- **Coconut Pit** - Growing medium for horticulture

---

## Integrated Processing Flow

### Complete Coconut Utilization Process:

1. **Coconut Water** → Concentrated (frozen) via steam evaporation
2. **Coconut Meat** → Cream extraction → Additional oil from pressed pulp
3. **Coconut Husk** → Oil extraction → Activated carbon production
4. **Waste Materials** → Charcoal dust → Boiler fuel → Steam generation

**Result:** Zero-waste operation where every part serves a purpose
