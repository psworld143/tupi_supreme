# Company Separation Analysis

---

## Executive Summary

**TSACI (Tupi Supreme Activated Carbon, Inc.)** and **TSUCOVI (Tupi Supreme Coco Ventures Incorporated)** are **separate business entities** with distinct operations, products, markets, and business models, yet they operate in an **integrated, synergistic manner** that maximizes coconut resource utilization.

## Integration & Synergy

### How They Work Together:

1. **Shared Resource: Coconut**
   - Both companies utilize different parts of the same raw material
   - Complete coconut utilization across both entities

2. **By-Product Exchange**
   - **TSUCOVI** processes coconut meat, water, and oil
   - **TSUCOVI** provides coconut husk (by-product) to **TSACI**
   - **TSACI** processes husk into activated carbon and growing mediums

3. **Zero-Waste Philosophy**
   - **"We Use All Parts of the Coconut"**
   - Maximizes value from every part
   - Sustainable, integrated operations

4. **Waste Utilization**
   - **TSACI** uses waste charcoal dust as boiler fuel
   - Steam generation powers operations
   - Complete resource optimization
