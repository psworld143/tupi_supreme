## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 Project Overview
Develop a professional, B2B-focused website for Tupi Supreme Coco Ventures Incorporated (TSUCOVI) that positions the company as a premium manufacturer of coconut food products with strict quality specifications for the food & beverage industry.

### 1.2 Website Objectives
1. **Establish Premium Brand:** Position TSUCOVI as quality coconut product manufacturer
2. **Generate Qualified Leads:** Attract food & beverage manufacturers and retail brands
3. **Showcase Product Specifications:** Highlight quality standards (30% fat, 15:1 ratio, -18°C)
4. **Build Credibility:** Demonstrate food-grade quality, certifications, manufacturing expertise
5. **Support Sales:** Provide product specifications, technical data, pricing inquiries
6. **Export Market Presence:** Facilitate international inquiries and partnerships

### 1.3 Success Metrics
- Lead generation (inquiries, quote requests)
- Product specification downloads
- International inquiry volume
- Time on product pages
- Bounce rate improvement
- Search engine rankings for food industry terms

### 2.1 Primary Audience

#### Food & Beverage Manufacturers (KEY TARGET)
- **Role:** Product developers, R&D managers, procurement officers, quality managers
- **Needs:** Consistent product specifications, food-grade quality, reliable supply, competitive pricing
- **Pain Points:** Quality consistency, specification compliance, supply chain reliability

#### Food Service Companies
- **Role:** Chefs, menu developers, procurement
- **Needs:** Culinary-grade products, consistent quality, bulk packaging

#### Export Markets / International Distributors

#### **Server Requirements:**
- Apache/Nginx web server
- PHP 7.4 or higher
- SSL certificate (HTTPS required)
- Email functionality
- File storage for document downloads

### 7.2 Performance Requirements

- **Page Load Time:** < 3 seconds
- **Mobile Performance:** Optimized for mobile networks
- **Image Optimization:** Compressed, WebP format
- **CDN:** For static assets (CSS, JS, images)
- **Caching:** Browser caching, server-side caching

### 7.3 SEO Requirements

#### **On-Page SEO:**
- Title tags (unique per page)
- Meta descriptions
- Header tags (H1-H6 structure)
- Alt text for images
- Internal linking structure
- URL structure (clean, descriptive)

#### **Keywords Focus:**
- Primary: "coconut cream 30% fat", "coconut water concentrate"
- Secondary: "frozen coconut products", "coconut oil manufacturer"
- Long-tail: "coconut cream for food manufacturing", "coconut water concentrate 15:1 ratio"
- Industry: "food-grade coconut products", "B2B coconut manufacturer"

#### **Technical SEO:**
- XML sitemap
- Robots.txt
- Structured data (Schema.org for food products)
- Mobile-friendly (Google requirement)
- Fast loading times
- SSL certificate

### 7.4 Security Requirements

- **HTTPS:** SSL certificate required
- **Form Security:**
  - CSRF protection
  - Input sanitization
  - XSS prevention
  - File upload security (if applicable)
- **Email Security:**
